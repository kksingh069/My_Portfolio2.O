# Storytelling Philosophy

The portfolio is not just a website.

It is a cinematic digital narrative experience that progressively reveals:
- personality
- creative philosophy
- technical craftsmanship
- design thinking
- motion direction
- visual identity

The visitor journey should feel:
- immersive
- atmospheric
- emotionally paced
- intentional
- cinematic

The website should feel like entering a creative universe rather than browsing a normal portfolio.

---

# Narrative Flow

The website experience should follow a cinematic progression:

1. Curiosity
2. Immersion
3. Discovery
4. Technical Depth
5. Creative Identity
6. Emotional Connection
7. Final Impact

Each section should transition naturally into the next like scenes in a film.

- emotional pacing
